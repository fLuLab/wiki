# Seminars and Conferences
Please see below for a list of local/online seminar series and frequently attended external conferences.

## 1. Seminars

Seminars are a good way to keep up with the field and meet people. Internal series are listed first, followed by external series that are open to us. Times and sign-up details change each term, so please check each series' own page.

### 1.1 Virus Club

**Topic:** An informal, (previously) regular meeting where group members and colleagues present work in progress in virus evolution.

**Details:** Traditionally once a month on thursday afternoon. Now Ad-hoc.

**Sign-up:** TBC

### 1.2 Internal (and External) Virology Seminars

**Topic:** Talks on virology from local researchers and invited external speakers.

**Details:** Monday 13.00-14.00

**Sign-up:** Ask Dr Rute Pinto to be added to the mailing list.


### 1.3 Phylo-Modelling Group

**Topic:** A discussion group on phylogenetic and phylodynamic modelling, covering methods, software (e.g. BEAST, see section 5) and problems members are working on.

**Details:** We aim at monthly mettings on a Wednesday; however, events sometimes intercede.

**Sign-up:** subcscribe to phylo-modelling-group on SYMPA

### 1.4 Juniper Seminar Series (External)

**Topic:** Seminars from Juniper, the Cambridge-based infectious disease modelling consortium that also organises IDDconf (section 2.3). Talks cover mathematical and statistical modelling of infectious diseases.

**Sign-up:** [*\[Link to Juniper seminar page / mailing list\]*](https://juniper.maths.org/seminars)

### 1.5 Epinowcast Seminar Series (External)

**Topic:** Seminars from the Epinowcast community on nowcasting and real-time estimation of infectious disease dynamics (reporting delays, right-truncated data, reproduction numbers). The community develops the R package [`epinowcast`](https://epinowcast.org/).

**Sign-up:** *[Link to Epinowcast seminar page; start from [epinowcast.org](https://epinowcast.org/)]*

### 1.7 Statistics Seminars (School of Mathematics)

**Topic:** Seminars on statistical theory, methodology and applications, including Bayesian inference and computational statistics, which are relevant to our modelling work.

**Details:** Monday afternoons

**Sign-up:** Subscribe to stats-seminars on SYMPA

### 1.8 IFEEAI Seminars (External)

**Topic:** The International Forum on Ecology and Evolution of Avian Influenza (IFEEAI) is a bi-weekly webinar series to discuss recent advances in the ecology and evolution of avian influenza viruses (both low pathogenic and highly pathogenic to domestic and wild animals and humans).

**Sign-up:**  Cotact Dr. Shenglai Yin (shenglai.yin@ou.edu) for registration

## 2. Conferences and Symposia

Dates and venues below were checked in October 2026 but are subject to change, so please confirm on each conference website. Where the most recent edition has already taken place and the next is not yet announced, this is stated, and you can check the website or join the mailing list.

### 2.1 Virus Genomics, Evolution and Bioinformatics

**Topic:** A hybrid meeting on viral genomics and evolution, covering virus dynamics within and between hosts, genomics and public health, and bioinformatics including machine learning and AI. Organised by Wellcome Connecting Science.

**Next:** 11–13 November 2026, Wellcome Genome Campus, Hinxton, UK (in person and virtual). The late in-person registration deadline is 12 October 2026 and the virtual deadline is 2 November 2026.

**Website and registration:** [Virus Genomics, Evolution and Bioinformatics 2026](https://coursesandconferences.wellcomeconnectingscience.org/event/virus-genomics-evolution-and-bioinformatics-20261111/)

### 2.2 Dynamics and Evolution of Human Viruses

**Topic:** A long-running meeting for quantitative and computational approaches to viral dynamics (within-host and cellular modelling) and to viral evolution and population genetics. Many methods originated in HIV research and are now applied to other viruses such as SARS-CoV-2.

**Next:** The 33rd meeting was held 19–22 May 2026 at UBC Okanagan, Kelowna, Canada. The next meeting has not yet been announced.

**Website:** [dynamicsevolution.org](https://dynamicsevolution.org/)

### 2.3 IDDconf

**Topic:** A small (~100 people), community-driven conference for infectious disease modellers, with an emphasis on new and in-progress work. Talk selection is partly randomised, and training workshops run alongside. Organised by Juniper.

**Next:** The 2026 meeting (sixth edition) was held 7–9 July 2026 in Ambleside, UK. The next meeting has not yet been announced.

**Website:** [iddconf.org](https://iddconf.org/)

### 2.4 ModAH

**Topic:** Modelling in Animal Health: mathematical modelling, statistical inference and simulation of pathogen spread in animal populations. It is linked to the ModAH-HUB network and the ModAH Collection, an open repository of animal health models.

**Next:** The most recent conference was planned for 25–27 August 2026 in Nantes, France. The next edition has not yet been announced (previous editions were 2017, 2021 and 2024).

**Website:** [modah-hub.com](https://modah-hub.com/)

### 2.5 SVEPM

**Topic:** The annual meeting of the Society for Veterinary Epidemiology and Preventive Medicine: veterinary epidemiology, disease surveillance, risk analysis and modelling, with a strong early-career focus and bursaries available.

**Next:** 17–19 March 2027, Lingotto, Torino (Turin), Italy. Registration is expected to open in November.

**Website:** [svepm.org.uk](https://svepm.org.uk/)

### 2.6 SMBE

**Topic:** The annual meeting of the Society for Molecular Biology and Evolution: molecular evolution, population and medical genomics, phylogenetics and comparative genomics. From 2027 the annual meeting will alternate between a global meeting (odd years) and regional meetings (even years).

**Next:** 20–24 June 2027, Québec City Convention Centre, Québec City, Canada.

**Website:** [smbe.org](https://www.smbe.org/)

### 2.7 ISVEE

**Topic:** The International Symposium on Veterinary Epidemiology and Economics, a triennial global meeting covering veterinary epidemiology, animal health economics and One Health.

**Next:** 8–12 November 2027, Century City Conference Centre, Cape Town, South Africa.

**Website:** [isvee18.com](https://isvee18.com/)

### 2.8 Virus Bioinformatics

**Topic:** The International Virus Bioinformatics Meeting (ViBioM), which brings together virologists and bioinformaticians on viral genomics, annotation, evolution and computational methods. It is run through the European Virus Bioinformatics Center.

**Next:** The 2026 meeting was held 18–20 May 2026 at Vilnius University, Lithuania. The next meeting has not yet been announced.

**Website:** [EVBC events page](https://evbc.uni-jena.de/event/international-virus-bioinformatics-meeting-2026/)

### 2.9 Epidemics

**Topic:** The International Conference on Infectious Disease Dynamics, a large biennial meeting (500+ delegates) on modelling and statistical inference for infectious diseases, including within-host dynamics, phylodynamics, ecology, antimicrobial resistance and surveillance data. Associated with the journal *Epidemics*.

**Next:** Epidemics 11, 5–8 December 2027, Singapore Expo, Singapore. The abstract deadline is 18 June 2027.

**Website:** [Epidemics conference page (Elsevier)](https://www.elsevier.com/events/conferences/international-conference-on-infectious-disease-dynamics)
