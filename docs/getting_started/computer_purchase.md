# Computer Purchases

At the start of your PhD/Post-doc, you will have the opportunity to purchase computer(s). In fLu lab, we are almost exclusively users of UNIX-based operating systems (ie. Linux and MacOS), therefore we are best able to provide support if you also use these. Whichever hardware you choose to purchase, you should bear in mind that a computer purchased at the start of your PhD/Postdoc will likely be obsolete within 4 years. Therefore, once you have determined your budget it is advisable you select the best specification you can afford. Please note that, due to their integrated design, it is not viable to upgrade Apple products with new RAM/storage/CPU after purchase.

Apple products can be purchased through the XMA punchout in P&M. You may find it easier to build a specification on the Apple website first, then locate the exact product on XMA. If the exact specification is not listed, then you can request it from XMA directly. If your work involves statisitcal simulation (e.g. MCMC, pMCMC, ABC), and you anticipate running these algorithms locally, note that MacBook Air/Neo may not provide adequate compute.

If you would prefer to run a Linux-based OS then the University’s preferred supplier is HP; however, we have experience with a range of external providers if a bespoke solution is needed. The University will not provide support for Linux kernel OS, therefore you must be comfortable setting up and administering your device. In particular, you must be alert to distribution security announcements and be able to apply temporary patches to ensure your device is protected. 

If you purchase a mobile device, you must also purchase desktop accessories (screen, keyboard, mouse, raiser). You should also purchase an external back up drive (such as Western Digital or Samsung) that is formatted for your OS. If there is any push-back from finance, you should politely point out that we frequently work in remote locations where backing up to data to datastore is not feasible.  

Talk to others in the lab if you have any questions/want to discuss ideas!
